No account manager. No mystery production line. You brief me, I design it, and we make it better together.

Quick answers

Before we work together.

Do you only design, or do you help with concepts too?

Both. If you already know exactly what you want, I can focus on execution. If not, I can help develop the visual angle before designing.

How much do you charge?

I quote based on what you need and how often you publish. Book a consultation and I’ll recommend the simplest setup that makes sense.

Can we work together long-term?

Yes. Ongoing work is a good fit for creators who want consistent thumbnails and a designer who learns their channel over time.

What do you need from me to start?

Usually the video topic, working title, any useful assets, references you like, and context about your audience. We can fill in the gaps together.
